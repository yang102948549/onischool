Spotlight · Oni Record

Student records, written and checked in one table

Every December, Korean teachers write hundreds of short, rule-bound comments for each student's official record. Oni Record turns that into four steps. See the full tour.

  1. WriteType like a spreadsheet. Pick keywords for each student and let AI draft subject, activity and behavior comments.
  2. AssistKeyword chips give the teacher material for each sentence, so the draft stays in the teacher's own observations.
  3. CheckEach draft is checked against the official writing guidelines and NEIS byte limits, with before and after shown side by side.
  4. ApplyApproved text goes straight into the NEIS grid through the Oni browser extension. No Excel upload step.
Student data

Built so student records stay with the teacher

School records are among the most sensitive data a teacher handles. Oni apps are designed around that.

Local-first

Rosters, schedules and records are saved in a database on the teacher's own PC. Oni School does not run a server that stores student records.

Bring your own AI key

Teachers connect their own AI API key. The key is stored on their PC, and requests go from the app directly to the AI provider.

Teacher stays in control

AI only drafts and checks. Nothing reaches NEIS until the teacher selects it and applies it.

Honest scope

Oni Record is a test release. The NEIS extension currently supports cbe.neis.go.kr, with more regions to follow after verification.

About

Made by a teacher, tested in a real school

Oni School is run by a Korean middle school teacher who has spent years building classroom tools: course registration, proctor assignment, record writing and classroom games. Each Oni app started as a tool for one school and was rebuilt so any teacher can install it.

Our aim is simple: give teachers back the hours that school admin takes, without asking them to give up control of their students' data.
