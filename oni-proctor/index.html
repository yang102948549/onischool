02 / Schedule and rooms

The schedule decides which seats exist.

A seat is a place a proctor is needed: a room in a period, or a corridor zone. Register the rooms, add exam days, then type the subject for each period and grade.

1

Register rooms by gradeAdd classes one at a time or several at once, then corridor zones and special rooms.

2

Add exam days and periodsEach day is a card with its own number of periods.

3

Type the subject into each cellArrow keys move between cells and Enter moves down, like a spreadsheet.

4

Split a cell when classes differAdd a second subject and choose which rooms each one applies to. Nothing changes until you confirm.

How seats are madeA corridor seat is created only in periods that have an exam. If every subject in a period is self-study, no corridor seat is made.

03 / Mode and scoring

Decide what counts as a fair share.

Choose how rooms are staffed and how much each kind of duty weighs. The engine balances teachers on the score these weights produce.

1

Choose the proctoring modeRegular mode places one proctor per room plus corridor duty. 정/부감독 mode places a chief and an assistant, with rooms you mark as single-proctor.

2

Set the weightsBy default a classroom duty counts 2 points and a corridor duty 1. Any value of zero or more, including decimals, can be used.

3

Set reference countsLimits such as classroom duties over the whole exam or corridor duties in a day raise a warning when exceeded.

Good to knowReference counts are warnings, not limits: they never block an assignment. In chief and assistant mode every duty counts as 1 point.

04 / Teacher list

Conditions come from one table.

Bring in the list, then set each teacher’s subjects, homeroom and blocked classes with a click. A preview shows how columns were matched and which rows have problems.

1

Bring in the listType it, paste a table, open an Excel, CSV or TSV file, use a Google Sheets link, or search a school in Comcigan’s public data.

2

Click a chip to set conditionsSubjects (more than one allowed), one homeroom class, and any classes the teacher must not proctor.

3

Set the roleMost are regular teachers. Roles such as roaming or corridor-only change where that teacher may be placed.

Why it mattersA teacher is never placed in their own homeroom, in a blocked class, or in a period when their own subject is being examined. Those rules come from this table.

05 / Who can cover when

Tick the exceptions, not the whole grid.

Regular teachers are available in every period unless you say otherwise. Special teachers work the other way round: they are placed only in the periods you tick.

1

Special teachersPart-time lecturers, non-subject, roaming and corridor-only teachers are placed in the periods you tick, and are used in those periods whenever possible.

2

Seat preferenceClassroom and corridor, or corridor only. A “corridor first” teacher can still cover a classroom when proctors run short.

3

Unavailable periodsFor regular teachers, tick only what is not possible, such as a business trip or childcare hours, with the reason beside it.

06 / Automatic assignment

Rules first, then fairness.

One button works out the whole roster. Hard rules are never relaxed to fill a seat or to improve a score, and the same rules apply when you edit by hand.

Hard rules

Rules that never bend

  • No excluded teachers and no unavailable periods
  • No own homeroom and no blocked class
  • No period in which the teacher’s own subject is examined
  • One seat per teacher in a period, one teacher per seat
Priorities

Then, in this order

  1. Place special teachers in the periods set for them
  2. Fill as many seats as possible
  3. Respect seat preferences
  4. Narrow the gap between the highest and lowest score
  5. Bring everyone closer to the average
Time

About ten seconds

The solver works for roughly ten seconds and returns the best roster it found. It states whether that roster was proven optimal or not. You can cancel, and the previous result is kept.

Validation

Checked a second time

A separate checker validates every result. The summary shows seats filled, unassigned seats, count warnings and unmet placements.

A teacher’s score in regular mode is classroom duties × the classroom weight plus corridor duties × the corridor weight. “Optimal” refers to the conditions you entered: it does not mean that a condition missing from the input was taken into account.

Before you start

It runs on one PC,
without the internet.

The assignment engine is built into the app. Setting up, assigning, adjusting, saving and exporting all work offline, and nothing else needs to be installed.

What you prepare

The facts of the exam

Exam dates and periods, the subjects for each grade, the rooms, and the teacher list with subjects and homerooms.

What the app does not guess

Missing conditions

A homeroom, role or blocked class that is missing from your source is not filled in for you. The result is only as complete as the conditions you enter.

The internet is used only when you choose to import a teacher list or timetable from a Google Sheets link or from Comcigan’s public timetable data.

Your data, explained

Everything stays on your PC.
Imports are the only exception.

Based on the app’s documentation and source as of October 2026.

On this Windows PC

Exams and teachers

Each exam is saved as a file in the Windows user profile, separate from the program folder, together with the last good copy.

A damaged file is reopened from that copy and the app says so.

No account

No cloud sync

The app does not upload teacher or exam data. Syncing to a cloud is outside its scope.

To move an exam to another PC, export it as a JSON file.

Online imports

Only when you ask

Fetching a Google Sheets link or Comcigan’s public timetable data is a request you start.

It does not sign in to an account or keep syncing afterwards.

The engine

Calculated on the PC

The assignment engine runs inside the app. The roster is not sent to a server to be solved.

Automatic saving protects against a crash, not against a lost PC. Keep a JSON export as a backup.

Can I download it now?

Not yet. There is no public download. Release and licensing details will be published on this page when it is ready.

Does it need the internet?

No, for the core work. Assignment, adjustment, saving and export run offline. Importing from Google Sheets or Comcigan needs a connection.

What does “optimal” mean here?

It means no better roster exists for the conditions you entered. It does not check whether a real-world condition was left out of the input.

Can a seat stay empty after a run?

Yes. If no teacher is allowed to take a seat, it stays empty and is reported. The rules are not relaxed to fill it.

Are these the app’s real screens?
